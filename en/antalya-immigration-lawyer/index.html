Immigration Lawyer in Antalya

Residence permits, work permits, citizenship and deportation matters.

This field governs the entry, stay, employment and acquisition of citizenship in Turkey by persons who are not Turkish nationals. In Antalya, where a substantial foreign population resides, applications and disputes of this kind arise frequently.

Rules Applying to Foreign Nationals in Turkey

The principal legislation consists of the Law on Foreigners and International Protection no. 6458, the Turkish Citizenship Law no. 5901 and the International Labour Force Law no. 6735. The Presidency of Migration Management and the Ministry of Labour and Social Security are the competent authorities.

Residence permits are issued in several categories, including short term, family, student, long term and humanitarian. Each has its own conditions, duration and renewal procedure.

Turkish citizenship may be acquired through the general procedure, through marriage, or by way of the exceptional route, which includes the acquisition of immovable property at or above the threshold set by legislation. The threshold is revised from time to time, and the property must be registered with an annotation preventing its sale for three years.

In Summary

Applications in this area are administrative in nature and are decided largely on the documents submitted. Preparing the file completely at the outset avoids both refusals and the loss of statutory periods. The same procedural rules apply to files involving foreign nationals; for the general framework see our law office in Antalya.
