Practice Areas

Av. Mücahit Çelik – Avukat Mücahit Çelik HUKUK & DANIŞMANLIK olarak Antalya'da müvekkillerimize 8 farklı hukuk dalında profesyonel avukatlık ve danışmanlık hizmeti sunuyoruz.

Set out below are the nine areas of law in which the office advises and conducts litigation. Under each heading the scope of the area, the basic rules of procedure and any mandatory pre-action stage are summarised. The links under each heading lead to a fuller account. A dispute may engage more than one area, in which case the basis on which the file will be assessed is determined at the first meeting.

Family Law

Boşanma, nafaka, velayet ve mal paylaşımı davalarında uzman destek.

In family files the type of divorce, the facts on which alimony and custody claims rest, and the division of matrimonial property are assessed together. In an uncontested divorce the scope of the protocol is decisive; in a contested divorce, fault and the state of the evidence. Foreign judgments require recognition or enforcement to take effect in Turkey. Ayrıntılı bilgi

  • Contested divorce actions
  • Uncontested divorce
  • Alimony and custody claims
  • Division of matrimonial property
  • Recognition and enforcement of foreign judgments
  • Compensation for broken engagement

Employment Law

İşçi-işveren uyuşmazlıkları, tazminat ve mobbing davalarında hukuki destek.

In employee claims the date of termination, the length of service and the gross salary including fringe benefits form the basis of the calculation. Severance and notice pay, overtime, weekly rest and annual leave entitlements are usually claimed in the same file. Mediation is mandatory before such claims may be brought. Ayrıntılı bilgi

  • Severance and notice pay claims
  • Reinstatement claims
  • Unpaid wages and overtime
  • Workplace harassment claims
  • Determination of unregistered service
Read More →

Real Estate and Tenancy Law

Tahliye, kira tespiti, tapu ve imar uyuşmazlıklarında profesyonel çözümler.

Grounds for eviction are set out exhaustively in the legislation, and each is subject to its own conditions and time limits. Determination of rent, adaptation, title deed cancellation and registration and partition of co-owned property are the other headings in this area. Tenancy disputes also require mediation before proceedings. Ayrıntılı bilgi

  • Eviction actions
  • Rent determination and adaptation
  • Title deed cancellation and registration
  • Partition of co-owned property
  • Construction in return for land share
Read More →

Criminal Law

Ağır ceza, soruşturma ve kovuşturma süreçlerinde etkin savunma.

In criminal files the statement given at the investigation stage shapes everything that follows. Assistance of counsel is generally optional, but mandatory in the situations listed in the legislation. The route and time limits for objecting to a detention order are assessed separately. Ayrıntılı bilgi

  • Assize Court proceedings
  • Criminal Court proceedings
  • Cybercrime
  • Attendance at investigation and questioning
  • Objections to detention
  • Criminal proceedings after traffic accidents
Read More →

Administrative Law

İdari işlemlere karşı iptal, tam yargı ve kamulaştırma davaları.

In annulment actions against administrative acts the period runs from service of the act and is, as a rule, sixty days. Shorter periods apply in certain fields. Loss caused by an act or conduct of the administration may be claimed through a full remedy action. Ayrıntılı bilgi

  • Annulment actions
  • Full remedy actions
  • Civil service disputes
  • Expropriation actions
  • Municipal and zoning disputes
Read More →

Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law

İcra takipleri, kambiyo işlemleri ve alacak tahsil süreçleri.

Whether proceedings are based on a judgment or on a document determines the route to be followed. An objection to the payment order stays the proceedings, and an application to the court is then required to annul or set aside the objection. The procedure for negotiable instruments runs to shorter periods. Ayrıntılı bilgi

  • Enforcement with and without judgment
  • Provisional attachment
  • Cheque and promissory note collection
  • Actions to annul an objection
Read More →

Consumer Law

Tüketici hakları, ayıplı mal ve hizmet uyuşmazlıkları.

The amount in dispute determines whether the application is made to the consumer arbitration committee or to the consumer court. Defective goods and services, distance contracts and vehicle diminished value claims are the headings most often seen. Time limits differ according to whether the defect was apparent or hidden. Ayrıntılı bilgi

  • Consumer court claims
  • Vehicle diminished value applications
  • Defective goods and services claims
Read More →

Commercial Law

Şirket hukuku, ticari sözleşmeler ve ticari uyuşmazlıklar.

Company formation, drafting of articles of association, share transfers and disputes between shareholders fall within this area. Mediation is mandatory for monetary claims and compensation in commercial matters. Careful drafting before a dispute arises narrows the scope of later argument. Ayrıntılı bilgi

  • Company formation
  • Commercial contracts
  • Shareholder disputes
  • Commercial litigation
Read More →

Immigration Law

Oturma izni, çalışma izni, vatandaşlık ve sınır dışı işlemleri.

Residence and work permit applications are administrative processes decided largely on the documents submitted. Turkish citizenship through the general route, through marriage and through the exceptional route involving property acquisition are subject to separate conditions. The period for challenging a deportation decision before the administrative court is short. Ayrıntılı bilgi

  • Residence permits for foreign nationals
  • Work permits for foreign nationals
  • Turkish citizenship applications
  • Appeals against deportation decisions
  • Property transactions by foreign nationals
  • Recognition and enforcement
