Legislative Bulletin

Amendments published in the Official Gazette and their effect in practice.

This page collects, in date order, the amendments published in the Official Gazette that bear on the office's fields of work. Each entry states the law that introduced the change, the date and issue of the Official Gazette, the date of entry into force and any transitional rule; the detailed assessment appears in the article itself.

Law no. 7593 — Official Gazette, 18 August 2026 (S. 33344)

Knife Carrying and Sales Restrictions Under Law No. 7593

Law no. 7593 introduced administrative fines of TRY 5,000-10,000 for carrying and selling knives, plus forfeiture, an age limit and a right of challenge.

2026-08-30 · Criminal Law

Law no. 7584 — Official Gazette, 20 June 2026 (S. 33286)

Litigation Costs and Land Registry Charges in De Facto Expropriation

Law no. 7584 amended article 29 of the Expropriation Law: land registry charges on registration and settlement are now payable by the administration.

2026-08-30 · Administrative Law
Read the article →
Law no. 7589 — Official Gazette, 31 July 2026 (S. 33326)

Türkiye's 12th Judicial Package: Key Changes Under Law No. 7589

Law no. 7589 abolished the unquantified claim action, tied statutory interest to the rediscount rate and set new rules for partition auctions and hearings.

2026-08-25 · Legislation and Current Developments
